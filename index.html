01 / ABOUT ME

MY JOURNEY INTO CLOUD & DEVOPS

Curious about how infrastructure works. Driven to make it better.

I'm an IT professional building on my infrastructure operations experience to move towards Cloud and DevOps Engineering.

My professional experience has given me exposure to enterprise IT operations, including monitoring and first-level troubleshooting across Linux servers, Windows servers and network devices. It helped me understand the importance of system availability, clear troubleshooting steps and reliable day-to-day operations.

I'm now developing deeper skills in AWS, cloud infrastructure, automation and CI/CD. I learn by building practical projects, documenting what I implement and revisiting the underlying concepts so I can explain the decisions behind each solution—not just the commands used.

My current focus is to strengthen my hands-on ability with Linux, Docker, Terraform and AWS, then bring those skills together in more complete infrastructure and deployment workflows.
